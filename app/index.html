Follow the steps for your device below.

How to install

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open kitreel.nedwebservices.com in Safari (or Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open kitreel.nedwebservices.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install.

Windows, Mac & Chromebook (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open kitreel.nedwebservices.com in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. KitReel 2.0 opens in its own window.

No icon? In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu → Cast, save and share → Install page as app. In Edge, open the … menu → Apps → Install this site as an app.

Mac (Safari)

  1. Open kitreel.nedwebservices.com in Safari.
  2. Choose File → Add to Dock.

Needs macOS Sonoma or newer.

Firefox & other browsers

Most of these can't install apps yet. Open KitReel 2.0 in Chrome, Edge or Safari, then follow the steps above. KitReel 2.0 works best on a computer with Chrome or Edge.
